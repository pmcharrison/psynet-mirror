In this particular experiment we need additional consent from you, regarding the use of audiovisual recordings.

Confidentiality

All the recordings we obtain during this research will be kept confidential, and nobody outside the group of researchers will be able to hear/see them. Your recordings will not be associated with your name or other identifiers in any way. Your recordings will not be used to derive your real identity, and they will not be made public. Raw audio/video will not be shared with collaborators (we will only share extracted data from recordings without personal identifiers).

Security

During data collection, audio-visual material will be recorded on a separate server and encrypted using strong encryption protocol (such as Advanced Encryption Standard). MTurk ID information will not be used to index this data.

Disclaimer

Although researchers will not use your recordings to derive your real identity, personal information could be leaked if you are not in an isolated environment. You may wish to make sure that no sounds/visual material in your environment could be linked to your personal information.

Consent

By consenting to participate, you acknowledge that you are 18 years or older, have read this consent form, agree to its contents, and agree to take part in this research. If you do not wish to consent, please close this page and return the HIT on Mechanical Turk.
