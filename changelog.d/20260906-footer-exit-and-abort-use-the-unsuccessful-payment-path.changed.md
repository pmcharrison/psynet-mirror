Footer Exit opens an in-page confirmation whose copy follows the actual payment outcome (Prolific screen-out with or without top-up, Prolific return-for-bonus, MTurk HIT submission, Lucid panel return, or unpaid local/generic leave), including concrete currency amounts where PsyNet can compute them. Below ``min_accumulated_reward_for_abort``, paid recruiters offer **Leave without payment** instead of blocking Exit; that path saves responses and records that no PsyNet payment is due. Error-page recovery always uses the compensated/plain leave pathway. Experiments can still customize the dialog through ``Experiment.early_exit_confirmation``. The obsolete ad-page abort popup and route remain removed.
