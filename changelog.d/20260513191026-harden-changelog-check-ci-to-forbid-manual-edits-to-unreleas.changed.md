Hardened the `changelog_check` GitLab CI job to forbid manual edits to the `## Unreleased` section of `CHANGELOG.md`. The job now (1) extracts the Unreleased block and fails if any non-marker, non-blank line is present, and (2) still requires the MR to add or delete a changelog fragment. This prevents contributors (or stale local rebuilds) from re-introducing the merge-conflict surface that fragments are designed to eliminate. (author: [Frank Höger])
