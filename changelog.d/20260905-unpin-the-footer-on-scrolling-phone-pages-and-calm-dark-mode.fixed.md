On a narrow screen (480px or less) whose page already scrolls, the participant footer now sits at the end of the content instead of staying pinned to the bottom of the window, so it no longer spends a fifth of a phone screen on chrome the participant scrolls past anyway. Pages that fit, and wider windows, keep the pinned footer. In dark mode, solid buttons use a new `--psynet-accent-solid` fill with a white label rather than the accent itself, which made a button the brightest thing on the page; the accent stays light for link text, where it needs to read against a dark surface.
