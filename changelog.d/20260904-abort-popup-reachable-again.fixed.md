Fixed the abort pages, whose buttons had become unreachable. Applying the content surface to them made the page taller than the popup window they open in, and that popup was created with `scrollbars=no`, so the buttons could be neither seen nor scrolled to. The abort pages now use a plain container rather than the content surface, lay their buttons out as a flex row instead of opposing floats, and the popup is opened with a workable size and with scrolling enabled.
