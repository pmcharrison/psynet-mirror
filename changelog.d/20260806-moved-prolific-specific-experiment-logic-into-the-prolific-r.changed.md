Moved Prolific-specific experiment logic into the Prolific recruiter class. The Prolific error-page content is now generated by the recruiter's `error_page_content` hook (the same mechanism already used by the Lucid recruiter), and `Experiment.error_page_content__prolific` has been removed; experiments that overrode it should override `Experiment.error_page_content` or `error_page_content` on a custom Prolific recruiter instead (stale overrides are detected by a pre-deployment check with migration instructions). Exit completion-code selection and post-submission accounting are likewise delegated to recruiter hooks (`exit_code_type` and `on_recruiter_submission_complete`): screen-out participants have their recorded base payment corrected to the fixed screen-out reward and are labeled `screened_out` rather than `approved`. `Experiment.bonus` computes the bonus uniformly as the total reward owed minus the base payment already paid by the platform (never negative), consulting the recruiter for the screen-out inputs and applying the base-payment correction before spend-cap checks so those checks see the true amount.
