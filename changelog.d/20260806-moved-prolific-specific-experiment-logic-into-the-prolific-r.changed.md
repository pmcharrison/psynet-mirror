Moved Prolific-specific experiment logic into the Prolific recruiter class. The Prolific error-page content is now generated by the recruiter's `error_page_content` hook (the same mechanism already used by the Lucid recruiter), and `Experiment.error_page_content__prolific` has been removed; experiments that overrode it should override `error_page_content` on a custom Prolific recruiter instead. Exit completion-code selection, screen-out bonus adjustment, and base-payment correction are likewise delegated to recruiter hooks (`exit_code_type`, `unsuccessful_participant_bonus`, and `on_recruiter_submission_complete`).
