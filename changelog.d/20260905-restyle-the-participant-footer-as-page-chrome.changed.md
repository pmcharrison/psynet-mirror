Restyled the participant footer as page chrome rather than content. It now has its own blue tint (`--psynet-footer-bg`, defined for light and dark mode) instead of sharing the white content surface, set deeper than the page background so that the bar keeps its own identity and its surface-filled controls read against it. Its contents line up with the page's content column rather than being spread across the whole window by Bootstrap's navbar spacing, with the reward on the left and the controls grouped on the right. The reward is a borderless figure with a small information glyph, matched in height to the buttons beside it, and `Comment` and `Exit` are outlined in the accent and danger colours so that their boundaries clear the 3:1 contrast WCAG asks of a control's edge. Recolouring the footer means setting `--psynet-footer-bg`, and `--psynet-danger-soft` is available for quiet danger surfaces.
