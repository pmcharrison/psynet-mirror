Participant payment is now decided, recorded, and transferred as separate steps. Recruiters implement `decide_payment` (returning a `PaymentDecision`) and `record_payment`; `Experiment.on_recruiter_submission_complete` records status and platform base first, then pays the bonus, instead of calling Dallinger's handler and patching screen-out amounts afterwards. A recruiter `on_recruiter_submission_complete` hook is not used; customize `decide_payment` and `record_payment` instead. `Experiment.bonus` raises `NotImplementedError`; customize `decide_payment` (or `platform_base_for` / `total_owed`) on the recruiter instead. Payout is tracked with `participant.payment_settled` so a retry can finish paying after status has already been recorded, and `reward_bonus` returning `False` leaves the participant unsettled. Spend caps are applied by `apply_payment_caps`: exceeding `hard_max_experiment_payment` withholds the bonus and records it on `participant.unpaid_bonus`, and `max_participant_payment` still clips per participant. Hard-cap projection is `amount_spent() + bonus`; `amount_spent` already includes the study base reserved for in-progress participants, so a separate `outstanding_base_payments` term is not used. Soft experiment spend limits remain a recruitment stop (`need_more_participants`), not a bonus-time email. PsyNet no longer runs Dallinger's `data_check` / `attention_check` hooks (`bad_data` / `did_not_attend`); experiments that override those hooks or `bonus` fail at deploy with migration guidance.
