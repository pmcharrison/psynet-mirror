Participant payment is now decided, recorded, and transferred as separate steps. Recruiters implement `decide_payment` (returning a `PaymentDecision`) and `record_payment`; `Experiment.on_recruiter_submission_complete` always re-records status and platform base first, then pays the bonus, instead of calling Dallinger's handler. A recruiter `on_recruiter_submission_complete` hook is not used. `Experiment.bonus` raises `NotImplementedError`; customize `decide_payment` (or `platform_base_for` / `total_owed`) on the recruiter instead. The decided Prolific top-up is stored as `participant.planned_bonus`, with `participant.bonus_status` of `not_due_yet`, `unconfirmed`, `success`, `dismissed`, or `capped`. Dallinger's `participant.bonus` remains the recorded (delivered) transfer used by `amount_spent()`. PsyNet posts a bonus automatically at most once per participant; `reward_bonus` returning `False` leaves status `unconfirmed` and continues recruitment. The Participants dashboard lists unconfirmed bonuses as participant id, worker id, and planned bonus. Opening a participant polls Prolific (`bonus_payments` may lag a POST), shows identity, progress, payment, and platform status in one table, and offers Pay bonus or Dismiss without paying when status is unconfirmed. Payment rows include issued completion code, base payment, planned bonus, delivered bonus, and bonus status. Unconfirmed planned bonus and bonus status are highlighted. The list banner is the only warning. That page stacks lookup and detail panels on small screens. The completion code actually issued is stored on `participant.issued_completion_code_type` so later payment matches what the platform paid. Spend caps are applied by `apply_payment_caps`: exceeding `hard_max_experiment_payment` now withholds the bonus (previously the cap emailed but still paid) and records it as `planned_bonus` with status `capped`; the cap is evaluated per payout against current `amount_spent()`, not latched after the first withhold. `max_participant_payment` still clips per participant. Hard-cap projection is `amount_spent() + bonus`; `amount_spent` already includes the study base reserved for in-progress participants. Soft experiment spend limits remain a recruitment stop (`need_more_participants`). PsyNet no longer runs Dallinger's `data_check` / `attention_check` hooks; experiments that override those hooks or `bonus` fail at deploy with migration guidance.
