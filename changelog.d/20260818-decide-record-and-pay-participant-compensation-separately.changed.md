Participant payment is now decided, recorded, and transferred as separate steps. Recruiters implement `decide_payment` (returning a `PaymentDecision`) and `record_payment`; `Experiment.on_recruiter_submission_complete` records status and platform base first, then pays the bonus, instead of calling Dallinger's handler and patching screen-out amounts afterwards. PsyNet no longer runs Dallinger's `data_check` / `attention_check` hooks (`bad_data` / `did_not_attend`); experiments that override them fail at deploy with migration guidance to `fail()` or `UnsuccessfulEndPage`.
