Participant payment is now decided, recorded, and transferred as separate steps. Recruiters implement `decide_payment` (returning a `PaymentDecision`) and `record_payment`; `Experiment.on_recruiter_submission_complete` always re-records status and platform base first, then pays the bonus, instead of calling Dallinger's handler. A recruiter `on_recruiter_submission_complete` hook is not used. `Experiment.bonus` raises `NotImplementedError`; customize `decide_payment` (or `platform_base_for` / `total_owed`) on the recruiter instead. Payout is tracked with `participant.payment_settled`. PsyNet posts a bonus automatically at most once per participant; `reward_bonus` returning `False` leaves the participant unsettled, stores the amount on `participant.unpaid_bonus`, sets `participant.needs_payment_review`, and continues recruitment. The Participants dashboard lists everyone who needs that review as participant id, worker id, and missing bonus. Opening a participant polls Prolific (`bonus_payments` may lag a POST), shows PsyNet status beside the platform status, and offers Pay bonus or Dismiss without paying. That page stacks lookup and detail panels on small screens. The completion code actually issued is stored on `participant.issued_completion_code_type` so later payment matches what the platform paid. Spend caps are applied by `apply_payment_caps`: exceeding `hard_max_experiment_payment` now withholds the bonus (previously the cap emailed but still paid) and records it on `participant.unpaid_bonus`; the cap is evaluated per payout against current `amount_spent()`, not latched after the first withhold. `max_participant_payment` still clips per participant. Hard-cap projection is `amount_spent() + bonus`; `amount_spent` already includes the study base reserved for in-progress participants. Soft experiment spend limits remain a recruitment stop (`need_more_participants`). PsyNet no longer runs Dallinger's `data_check` / `attention_check` hooks; experiments that override those hooks or `bonus` fail at deploy with migration guidance.
