Added automatic payment of unsuccessful (failed or errored) Prolific participants via a fixed screen-out completion code. Setting the new `prolific_unsuccessful_base_payment` config parameter registers an `UNSUCCESSFUL` completion code with Prolific's `FIXED_SCREEN_OUT_PAYMENT` action; unsuccessful participants then submit normally, receive the fixed payment from Prolific, and are topped up to their accumulated reward with a bonus (controlled by `prolific_unsuccessful_topup`, with the screen-out budget controlled by `prolific_screen_out_slots`). The Prolific error page now offers such participants a submit button instead of asking them to message the experimenter. The previous return-for-bonus flow remains the fallback when the parameter is unset.
