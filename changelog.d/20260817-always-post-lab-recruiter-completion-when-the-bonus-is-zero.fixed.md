Submission outcomes now pass through a recruiter hook after payment is decided and capped. The default transfers real bonuses and settles sub-cent amounts without a platform call. Lab Recruiter reports every terminal outcome through its combined completion/payment endpoint, including a zero bonus. Calling ``reward_bonus`` on Lab Recruiter raises an error so that path is not confused with a bonus transfer. Local debug without a Lab Recruiter auth token skips the outcome POST instead of leaving the participant in payment review.
