Prolific confirmation pages now say the submission has been sent to Prolific, and they no longer show a progress bar or reward footer. The error page uses a single heading, "An error occurred", then one paragraph that begins "Unfortunately an error occurred and we cannot continue." When responses were saved, that paragraph continues with "However, your responses so far have been saved," plus recruiter-specific next steps such as paying for progress through Prolific. Recruiter-exit Submit tells participants they will not need a completion code, and shows a wait state and a Try again path. Terminal pages use "You may close this page." Lucid error, consent-reject, and debrief copy all talk about returning to the panel. Error, exit, and Lucid tab titles follow the page heading.
