On a phone (480px or less) the participant footer now follows the content instead of staying pinned to the bottom of the window: it comes to rest at the bottom edge of the window or at the end of the page, whichever is lower. A page that fits therefore looks as it did before, while a longer one gives its whole window to the content rather than spending a fifth of a phone screen on chrome the participant scrolls past anyway. Wider windows keep the pinned footer, with room reserved for its measured height; that amount is now also available to templates as `--psynet-footer-reserved`, which is zero wherever the footer does not overlap the content. In dark mode, solid buttons use a new `--psynet-accent-solid` fill with a white label rather than the accent itself, which made a button the brightest thing on the page; the accent stays light for link text, where it needs to read against a dark surface.
