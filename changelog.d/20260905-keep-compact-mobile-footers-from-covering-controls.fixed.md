The participant footer is now compact and can no longer cover the last control on a page. It shows the total reward and an `Exit` button, with the time and performance breakdown and an explanation of `Exit` available as tooltips on hover, keyboard focus, and to screen readers. The footer stays pinned to the bottom of the window, and the space reserved for it is measured from the footer PsyNet actually rendered (published as `--psynet-footer-height`), so a footer that wraps onto several rows on a narrow phone, in another language, or at a larger font size no longer leaves the response control permanently hidden underneath.
