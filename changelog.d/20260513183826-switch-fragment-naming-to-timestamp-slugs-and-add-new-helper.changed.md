Switched changelog fragment naming from MR-number IDs to a generalized timestamped-slug scheme (`<YYYYMMDDHHMMSS>-<slug>.<category>.md`) so filenames are unique across MRs and direct pushes without depending on MR numbers. Added a `--new <category> "<description>"` helper to `docs/scripts/build_changelog.py` that generates the filename, slugifies the description, and writes a stub fragment for editing. Existing numeric IDs (`9xxx` migration fragments, older `<MR>.<category>.md` entries) remain valid and will be consumed at the next release. (author: [Frank Höger])
