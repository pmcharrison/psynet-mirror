Participants can leave a study early through **Leave** in the timeline footer when enabled with ``show_early_exit_button``. Leave opens an in-page confirmation that states the payment consequence for the recruiter in use — Prolific screen-out with or without top-up, Prolific return-for-bonus, Lucid panel return, or leaving without payment — quoting concrete currency amounts where PsyNet can compute them, and offering a passive **Cancel** that preserves the page and its responses. When an error makes continuation impossible, PsyNet executes the corresponding error-recovery exit automatically instead of asking the participant to confirm a false choice. Voluntary and automatic exits both use a server-owned ``EarlyExitPlan`` stored on the participant, so the browser cannot select a cheaper outcome and the amounts shown for voluntary Leave are the amounts paid. Below ``min_reward_for_paid_early_exit``, paid recruiters offer **Leave without payment** instead of withholding the voluntary option, while automatic error recovery always uses the compensated path and can recover payment even when the reward cannot initially be calculated. PsyNet then advances to a dedicated release branch, which gives the recruiter's own return or submission instructions. Voluntary Leave is not offered once a participant is finishing, since leaving there could only reduce their payment. Experiments can customise the voluntary dialog by overriding ``Experiment.early_exit_plan`` and can customise automatic recovery through ``Experiment.error_recovery_early_exit_plan``.
