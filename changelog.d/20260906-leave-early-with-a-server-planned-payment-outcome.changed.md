Participants can leave a study early through **Leave** in the timeline footer (enabled with ``show_early_exit_button``) and on the error page. Leave opens an in-page confirmation that states the payment consequence for the recruiter in use — Prolific screen-out with or without top-up, Prolific return-for-bonus, MTurk HIT submission, Lucid panel return, or leaving without payment — quoting concrete currency amounts where PsyNet can compute them, and offering a passive **Cancel** that preserves the page and its responses. The confirmation and the payment that follows both come from a server-owned ``EarlyExitPlan`` stored on the participant, so the browser cannot select a cheaper outcome and the amounts shown are the amounts paid. Below ``min_reward_for_paid_early_exit``, paid recruiters offer **Leave without payment** instead of withholding the option, while error-page recovery always offers the compensated path and falls back to copy without a reward quote when the reward cannot be calculated. After confirmation PsyNet advances to a dedicated release branch, which gives the recruiter's own return or submission instructions. Leave is not offered once a participant is finishing, since leaving there could only reduce their payment. Experiments can customise the dialog by overriding ``Experiment.early_exit_plan`` (or ``Experiment.error_recovery_early_exit_plan``) and replacing the plan's confirmation.
