Participants can leave a study early through **Leave** in the timeline footer when enabled with ``show_early_exit_button``. Leave opens an in-page confirmation that states the payment consequence for the recruiter in use — Prolific screen-out with or without top-up, Prolific return-for-bonus, Lucid panel return, or leaving without payment — quoting concrete currency amounts where PsyNet can compute them, and offering a passive **Cancel** that preserves the page and its responses. When an error makes continuation impossible, PsyNet executes the corresponding error-recovery exit automatically instead of asking the participant to confirm a false choice and keeps the explanation visible. Generic, local, and lab participants can then close the page and receive an email contact with one reference code; Prolific participants see their payment outcome and submit directly to Prolific; Lucid participants return to their panel; and recruiter-specific failure text directs participants to the appropriate support channel. The legacy Prolific return-for-bonus path introduces its required payment steps. Voluntary and automatic exits both use a server-owned ``EarlyExitPlan`` stored on the participant, so the browser cannot select a cheaper outcome and the amounts shown for voluntary Leave are the amounts paid. Below ``min_reward_for_paid_early_exit``, paid recruiters offer **Leave without payment** instead of withholding the voluntary option, while automatic error recovery always uses the compensated path and can recover payment even when the reward cannot initially be calculated. Voluntary Leave is not offered once a participant is finishing, since leaving there could only reduce their payment. Experiments can customise the voluntary dialog by overriding ``Experiment.early_exit_plan`` and can customise automatic recovery through ``Experiment.error_recovery_early_exit_plan``.
