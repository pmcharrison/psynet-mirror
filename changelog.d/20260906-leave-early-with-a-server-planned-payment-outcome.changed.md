Participants can use **Leave** for a recruiter-specific early exit with clear payment or panel-return consequences. Successful, unsuccessful, voluntary, consent-rejection, and error-recovery outcomes now share a server-owned ``ExitPlan`` and ``PaymentDecision``, so final-page handoff and later payment settlement use the same recorded decision. Fatal errors execute recovery automatically, while voluntary Leave requires confirmation and remains unavailable once the participant is already finishing.
